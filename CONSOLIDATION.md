# CONSOLIDATION — antlist + nslink → ness

**Status:** binding brand decision  
**Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Canonical product:** [ness.city](https://ness.city) only

## Decision

**ness** is the product brand.

- **antlist** is consolidated into ness. Treat any antlist repo/folder as an **archive / idea source only**. Do not ship features, PRs, or deploys under the antlist brand.
- **nslink** is consolidated into ness. The router-scan idea lives (if at all) as a parked/secondary surface under ness; it is **not** a separate product roadmap and is **not** a revival of an nslink router bot product.

There is one public product loop and one public domain: **ness.city**.

## What ness is

Civic layer for builders. Independent of Network School (ns.com).

| Surface | Status | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Townhall** | Primary live loop | Problems → proposals → bounties → ship |
| **Jobs** | Live, hand-curated | Public openings, direct apply |
| **PageRank** | Live UI | Social ring / who the city named |
| **Match** | In design | Resume ↔ jobs/bounties |
| **Market** | Planned / parked | Products · services · assets |

## antlist — folded

| Topic | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Brand | **Retired.** Do not market or ship as antlist. |
| Useful ideas | Port into ness Jobs / directory / matching only if they serve the civic loop. |
| Roadmap | **Off.** No antlist milestones, no antlist PRs. |
| Code / sibling folders | Archives and reference only. Do not thrash sibling antlist worktrees for ness product work. |

## nslink — folded

| Topic | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Brand | **Retired as a product.** Ness is the brand. |
| Router scan tool | Legacy routes may redirect (`/nslink` → `/routers`). Not the primary loop. |
| Router bot revival | **Explicitly out of scope.** Do not revive nslink bot shipping as a parallel product. |
| Roadmap | **Off product roadmap.** Parked under ness tools if kept at all. |

## What stays on the ness roadmap

1. **Townhall E2E** — surface, vote, propose, fund, ship (strongest proof loop).
2. **Jobs trust** — curated listings, direct apply, weekly refresh.
3. **Evidence & offer clarity** — one-liner, receipts, retention metrics.
4. **Match / Market** — only after Townhall or Jobs shows a measurable usage loop.

## What is off the roadmap

- antlist as a product or brand
- nslink as a product or brand
- nslink router bot revival
- Rebranding ness as Network School
- Feature work in antlist / nslink sibling folders for this company
