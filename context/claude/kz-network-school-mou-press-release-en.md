The Ministry of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Development of the
Republic of Kazakhstan and the global Network School initiative have
signed a Memorandum of Understanding on strategic cooperation in the
fields of artificial intelligence, innovation, technological
entrepreneurship, and human capital development.

Network School brings together entrepreneurs, engineers, researchers, and
investors from around the world. Its founder, Balaji Srinivasan, is one of
Silicon Valley's best-known technology entrepreneurs, the former CTO of
Coinbase, and an investor in hundreds of technology companies.

The document was signed by Zhaslan Madiyev, Deputy Prime Minister of the
Republic of Kazakhstan and Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital
Development, and by Balaji Srinivasan, founder and CEO of Network School.

Signing the agreement, Zhaslan Madiyev noted: "Artificial intelligence
develops where there are strong people, bold ideas, and an environment
where they can be realized. We are building exactly that kind of ecosystem
in Kazakhstan: open to talent, to technology entrepreneurs, and to global
partnerships. Cooperation with Network School will help strengthen this
work further."

The memorandum defines the main directions of cooperation, including:
developing educational programs in artificial intelligence, supporting
startups, conducting joint research, developing international scientific
and technological cooperation, and attracting the world's leading
specialists, entrepreneurs, researchers, and investors to Kazakhstan.

One of the main directions will be the creation of Network School's first
campus in Kazakhstan. On its basis, the plan is to implement world-class
educational, research, and entrepreneurship programs, international
residency and fellowship programs, and to integrate the campus into
Kazakhstan's national AI ecosystem.

The parties also intend to jointly develop programs for startups, organize
international conferences, hackathons, and forums, and to explore the
possibility of holding the Network State Conference 2026 in Kazakhstan.

Special attention will be given to promoting Kazakhstan as a regional hub
for artificial intelligence and innovation, attracting international
technology companies, venture funds, and strategic investors, and
developing a favorable environment for technological entrepreneurship.

Within the framework of the cooperation, the parties will also consider
improving mechanisms for attracting international specialists and
technology companies, developing digital public administration, exchanging
global best practices in artificial intelligence, and implementing joint
strategic initiatives.

The memorandum is set for a 5-year term. The fulfillment of these
agreements is expected to strengthen Kazakhstan's international position
as one of the leading regional centers for the development of artificial
intelligence, innovation, and technological entrepreneurship.
