# OFFER — ness.city

Updated 2026-07-28.

## One-liner

For **community operators and ambitious builders** who struggle with **problems that stay invisible and unfunded**, **ness.city** is a **civic coordination layer** that turns **diagnosed problems into funded, shipped fixes with public attribution**. Unlike **chat threads, spreadsheets, and closed ops tickets**, we **make the full loop public: surface → propose → bounty (USDC) → ship**, with karma for solvers and permanent credit for patrons.

## Price

Free to use (MIT). Optional paid ops / white-label for multi-city operators — TBD after first retention proof.
